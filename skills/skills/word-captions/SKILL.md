# Word-by-word captions

- Timing from `cutroom transcribe` word timestamps of the *edited* speech; never guess.
- Two or three words per group, one line, max ~16 characters. Break at phrase ends, not mid-phrase.
- Heavy sans (800 weight), white with a dark outline or soft shadow, ~6% of frame height, centred at 68–72% of the height — clear of the face and of the platform's buttons.
- The word being spoken is highlighted (accent colour or scale 1.08); each group snaps in (no fades longer than 80 ms) and leaves when the next arrives.
- Build as one HyperFrames overlay for the whole video. Check three frames: no group covers the mouth, none runs past the safe margins.
