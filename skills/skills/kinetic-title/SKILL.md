# Kinetic title

- 2–5 words, upper case, heavy condensed sans, ~11% of frame height, centred or left-aligned on a clean area of the shot.
- Words enter one after another, 80–120 ms apart: scale 1.25 → 1 with a 6-frame overshoot ease (`back.out(2)`), no blur.
- Holds 1.2–2 s, then cuts out with the shot (no fade).
- One HyperFrames overlay. Pair with an impact sound on the last word if sound effects are available.
- Check a frame at the end of the entrance: text inside the safe margins, readable over the picture (add a 35% dark scrim behind if not).
