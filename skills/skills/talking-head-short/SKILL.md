# Talking-head short

**Format** 1080×1920, 30 fps, 20–45 s unless the user says otherwise.

**Structure**
1. Hook (0–3 s): the single most surprising or useful sentence in the footage, even if it was said late. No intro, no "hi guys".
2. Body: one idea. Keep only sentences that move it forward.
3. Payoff: end on the takeaway or the call to action, then stop. No fade-out.

**Cutting**
- Transcribe every clip first; choose takes from the transcript, cut on word timestamps.
- Remove pauses longer than ~0.3 s, filler words, restarts and repeated takes (keep the last good one).
- Leave ~0.08 s of air before the first word and after the last word of each cut so nothing sounds clipped.
- Hard cuts only. A new clip every 2–5 s keeps the rhythm.

**Captions** (one HyperFrames overlay for the whole video, timed from the word timestamps)
- 2–4 words on screen at a time, heavy sans-serif, white with a dark outline or soft shadow.
- Highlight the word being spoken (accent colour or slight scale pop).
- Place in the lower-middle third: clear of the face, and clear of the bottom 20 % where app UI sits.

**Graphics** Sparingly: an optional 1–2 line title over the hook, and the logo from assets/ small in a top corner if one exists.

**Audio** Voice first. Music only if provided, at 0.08–0.15 volume.

**Check before replying** Hook lands in the first 3 s · no cut clips a word · captions match the speech and stay inside the safe area.
