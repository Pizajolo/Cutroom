# Montage

**Format** Match the footage orientation. 30 fps. 20–60 s, or the length of the music section used.

**Selecting**
- Make contact sheets of every clip and look at all of them. Shortlist shots with clear subjects, movement, light or emotion; drop shaky, dark and duplicate shots.
- Vary scale: wide → medium → detail. Never put two near-identical shots next to each other.

**Structure**
1. Opening: an establishing or intriguing shot, held a little longer (2–3 s).
2. Build: shots get shorter as energy rises (2 s → 1 s).
3. Peak: the best moments, fastest cutting.
4. Landing: one calm closing shot, held 2–4 s.

**Cutting**
- With music: detect beats (hyperframes beats <file>) and place cuts on them; choose the section of the track with a clear build, using the audio item's "in".
- Without music: cut on movement, and keep natural sound at 0.5–0.8.

**Graphics** Minimal. An optional small title at the start (place, date or event name, only if the user supplied it) and nothing else. No captions.

**Audio** Music at 0.7–1.0, natural sound under it at 0.1–0.2 for texture; mute clips with distracting noise or chatter.

**Check before replying** Cuts fall on the beat · no shot outstays its welcome · the first and last shots are the strongest.
