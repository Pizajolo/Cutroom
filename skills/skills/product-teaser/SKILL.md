# Product teaser

**Format** Ask nothing, decide from the footage: vertical footage → 1080×1920, landscape → 1920×1080. 30 fps, 10–25 s.

**Structure**
1. Cold open (0–2 s): the most striking shot, with a 2–4 word title.
2. Three to five beats: one benefit each — a shot plus a short title. Verbs, not adjectives.
3. End card (2–3 s): logo from assets/ centred on a clean background, plus one line (name, tagline or call to action).

**Cutting**
- Shots of 0.8–2.5 s. Look at the footage and pick moments with motion; start each clip just before the action.
- If there is music, find its beats (hyperframes beats <file>) and cut on them.
- No speech required. If someone speaks, use at most one short line.

**Graphics** (HyperFrames overlays)
- Big, confident type: one typeface, two weights, tight tracking. Titles animate in quickly (≤ 0.3 s) and leave before the next cut.
- Take colours from the logo. One accent colour, used consistently.
- Keep titles away from the subject of the shot — check frames.

**Audio** Music drives it when available (0.6–0.9 volume when nobody speaks). Without music, keep natural sound low.

**Check before replying** Understandable with sound off · every title readable in its time on screen · logo sharp and correctly proportioned.
