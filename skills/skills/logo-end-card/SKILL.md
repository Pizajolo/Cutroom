# Logo end card

- Final clip or overlay, 1.5–2.5 s. Background: brand colour, or the last shot blurred and darkened.
- Logo from assets/ at 28–36% of the frame width, centred slightly above the middle; one line (URL or call to action) below at ~3.5% of frame height.
- Logo scales 0.94 → 1 and fades in over 300 ms; the line follows 150 ms later. Music tail fades out under it.
- Never stretch or recolour the logo; if there is none in assets/, ask for it rather than drawing one.
