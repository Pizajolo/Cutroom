Confirm the topic, audience knowledge, desired length and references. Use 16:9 unless another format is requested. Begin with the question and promised outcome; introduce the necessary context, explain a few ordered points, then summarize the answer. Keep chapter titles short and consistent. Support narration with relevant B-roll, diagrams or editable text compositions, not decorative footage that obscures the point. Let complex visuals remain on screen long enough to understand. Keep captions readable and music subordinate to narration. Preserve the source's meaning and identify unsupported statements rather than inventing facts. Close with a concise recap and an optional user-provided next step.
