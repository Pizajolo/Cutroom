Confirm the platform, target length and the conversation's central point. Find a complete exchange with enough context to stand alone. Open with a compelling line without misrepresenting the speaker, preserve the question when it explains the answer and remove only redundant pauses. Reframe for the confirmed destination while keeping speakers' faces clear; cut to the active speaker when footage supports it. Use consistent caption styling, short phrases and unobtrusive speaker labels when useful. Avoid excessive punch-ins, reaction shots from unrelated moments and music that competes with dialogue. End on a resolved thought rather than a mid-sentence cutoff. Ask before adding attribution or a call to action.
