---
name: Product teaser
description: A short product introduction with a visual hook, one benefit and a focused closing card.
---
Confirm the product, audience, destination format, length and available imagery. Structure the edit as a visual hook, a single benefit, supporting product details and a closing card. Use short, general headline patterns that can be replaced for each project. Keep typography consistent, align motion with the product's tone and leave enough time to read each claim. Prefer simple reveals and motivated cuts. Use licensed music with clear speech or title emphasis. Ask for evidence before including factual performance claims. Finish with the user's chosen call to action, not invented brand details.
