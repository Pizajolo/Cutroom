---
name: Talking-head short
description: A concise vertical story built around one speaker, readable captions and a clear takeaway.
---
Ask which platform, audience and takeaway matter before choosing a length. Default to a 9:16 composition only after confirming the destination.
Open with the strongest complete sentence, remove pauses without changing meaning, and build one clear idea. Keep the speaker visible; add relevant B-roll only when available. Use high-contrast captions in short phrases within platform safe areas. Use restrained title cards and clean cuts rather than constant transitions. Keep music optional and quiet beneath speech. End with a concise takeaway or requested call to action. Never invent quotations or source footage.
